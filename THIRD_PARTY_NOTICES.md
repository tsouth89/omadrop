The filenames preserve the author credits supplied with the source collection.
Some bundled originals match that snapshot byte for byte. Others normalize line
endings or change only projectM's `fRating` metadata.

MilkDrop presets were commonly released through community collections without
individual license terms. The projectM team
[documents the same history](https://github.com/projectM-visualizer/presets-cream-of-the-crop/blob/master/LICENSE.md)
for its current preset pack and offers to remove a preset when an author objects
to its inclusion. Omadrop follows that provenance, attribution, and removal
practice. This is not a claim that projectM owns these works or that their
public-domain status is legally settled.

These files are not covered by Omadrop's MIT License. They retain their original
authorship and rights.

`Omadrop + Aderrasi - Contortion (Reactive Tunnel Edition).milk` is an
Omadrop-authored derivative of the credited Aderrasi preset. It preserves the
original tunnel composition while adding distinct low, mid, and high-frequency
attack envelopes and authored geometric responses. The derivative retains the
same original authorship and rights notice.

`Omadrop + Aderrasi - Halls Of Centrifuge (Reactive Orbit Edition).milk` is an
Omadrop-authored derivative of the credited Aderrasi preset. It preserves the
central centrifugal composition and rectangular frame while adding distinct
low, mid, and high-frequency motion. The derivative retains the same original
authorship and rights notice.

`Omadrop + Martin - Wire Dance (Reactive Wire Edition).milk` is an
Omadrop-authored derivative of the credited Martin preset. It preserves the
looping filament composition while adding separate low, mid, and high-frequency
geometry and color responses. The derivative retains the same original
authorship and rights notice.

Rights holders can request an attribution correction or removal by opening an
issue at <https://github.com/btsouth/omadrop/issues>.
